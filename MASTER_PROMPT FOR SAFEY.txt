### 2. Product definition and boundaries

Safey helps people with regular, irregular, seasonal, freelance, or mixed income record money activity, understand their recorded position, explore “what if” plans with the **Future Self Simulator**, and see explainable signs of possible cash-flow pressure through the **Financial Early-Warning System**.

MVP is iOS and Android, Ghana first, adults 18+, English (Ghana), GHS / GH₵, and `Africa/Accra` defaults. The MVP data sources are manual entry and user-reviewed CSV import. Users may represent cash, manually tracked bank, manually tracked Mobile Money, and other sources. These are user-entered records, not connected accounts.

Do not implement or imply direct bank/Mobile Money connections, transaction synchronization, payment initiation, transfers, deposits, credit scoring, investment or loan advice, tax/accounting compliance, fraud detection, guaranteed forecasts, or provider coverage. Future connections are an evaluation gate only. A future provider must be identified and its current documented API, commercial availability, sandbox, supported coverage, consent/revocation, security, and legal requirements independently verified before implementation. Until then, keep integration points inert and clearly unimplemented. Never collect bank/MoMo PINs or OTPs.

AI is optional, cloud-powered, and only an explanation layer over deterministic financial calculations. It must not create or alter numeric facts, make financial decisions, or mutate records. Core ledger and planning must remain useful with AI disabled or unavailable.

### 6. Data and financial model

Follow `ARCHITECTURE.md`’s model and adjust only when repository realities require it. Preserve ownership, timestamps, versioning, and auditability. Expected domain entities include:

- `profiles`: auth user identity, locale, timezone, currency, eligibility confirmation metadata, and settings.
- `consents`: user, purpose, policy version, granted/revoked state, and timestamps. AI consent is separate, opt-in, and revocable.
- `sources`: user-owned label and type (`cash`, manually tracked `bank`, manually tracked `mobile_money`, `other`); no false connection status.
- `categories`: user-editable/seeded hierarchy and active state.
- `transactions`: owner, positive amount in GHS minor units, currency, direction/type, occurred-at timestamp, source, category, optional note, provenance/import reference, and created/updated timestamps.
- `obligations`: owner, label, positive amount/currency, due date, recurrence where supported, source/category as appropriate, and active state.
- `scenarios`: owner, template, validated input assumptions, horizon, engine version, result snapshot or reproducible input reference, and timestamps.
- `insights`: owner, rule ID/version, severity, time window, evidence references, coverage, status/dismissal/snooze state, and generated timestamp.
- `imports` / `import_rows`: owner, file metadata/checksum and lifecycle state, mapping, row validation/duplicate result, explicit confirmation, and idempotency key. Avoid retaining raw files longer than needed; document retention.
- `entitlements` / `subscriptions`: provider-neutral state and feature limits; never assume a billing provider or price.

Use integer minor units (pesewas) for GHS; validate positive bounded amounts and supported precision at every boundary. Never use floating point for money arithmetic. Specify rounding, date/timezone boundaries (`Africa/Accra` by default), and currency formatting centrally. Validate user input on client and server. Preserve source dates/currency values in the CSV review step and normalize only after user confirmation.

Implement a deterministic, typed finance engine separate from UI and AI. Given the same versioned inputs it must return the same outputs. It owns recorded summaries, balances, baseline/scenario projections, uncertainty/sensitivity, coverage checks, and warning-rule results. Store engine/rule version with saved outputs. Explain how opening balances and transactions combine; prevent double-counting opening balance and imported activity. Support irregular income and do not assume monthly salary cadence. For insufficient or stale records, show a qualified result or suppress it; never manufacture precision.

### 7. Supabase, APIs, and RLS

Use Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, RLS, and Edge Functions as proposed, unless an existing repository decision is documented. Check in versioned SQL migrations and typed client contracts. Never put service-role, AI, or other privileged secrets in the mobile app or source control.

Enable RLS on every user-owned table. Policies must scope select/insert/update/delete to the authenticated owner (`auth.uid()` or a safe ownership relationship), with server-only access tightly constrained. A client must not be able to change ownership fields, grant itself consent/entitlements, or bypass validation. Review views, RPCs, storage buckets, and Edge Functions for equivalent isolation. Add and run tests that attempt cross-user reads/writes, missing JWT access, and revoked-consent AI access. Do not claim RLS is secure merely because policies exist.

Keep API handlers authenticated, input-validated, versioned, rate-limited where abuse is possible, and idempotent for writes/import commits. Use pagination and explicit error shapes. Only server-side functions may call an AI vendor. AI tools are a small allowlist of read-only operations such as `get_computed_summary` and `explain_scenario`; no arbitrary SQL, arbitrary network calls, or write/payment tools. Treat descriptions, CSV cells, and notes as untrusted prompt content. Minimize and pseudonymize sent context, do not send free-text notes by default, validate structured AI output, escape rendered text, disclose AI-generated responses, and provide a useful deterministic fallback.

Do not select an AI, analytics, crash, push, or billing vendor by assumption. Make vendor adapters/configuration explicit, document the choice and verification needed, and keep optional capabilities disabled until credentials and privacy/vendor terms are configured. Do not claim retention, residency, provider training opt-out, legal compliance, or store approval until verified. Avoid storing prompts/raw financial content in logs.

### 8. CSV import

Keep uploaded file handling local or short-lived where possible; if remote temporary storage is necessary, document access and retention and delete it after the flow. Do not silently guess ambiguous dates, amount signs, or currencies.

### 15. Acceptance criteria

Consider the MVP ready for a controlled release only when the PRD’s acceptance criteria are satisfied:

1. An eligible Ghana-based adult can understand choices and use the app without AI or account connection.
2. GHS, dates, and manually entered sources are consistent and never described as live provider data.
3. Income/expenses can be added, corrected, categorized, filtered, and deleted.
4. CSV has preview, mapping, validation, duplicate review, explicit commit, idempotency, and recoverable errors.
5. Dashboard and simulator match the deterministic engine and show coverage, as-of time, and assumptions.
6. Every warning traces to a versioned rule and evidence; sparse/stale records do not create unqualified warnings.
7. AI is separately consented, server-side, read-only, grounded, and has deterministic fallback.
8. RLS blocks cross-user access on every user-owned table; sensitive keys are absent from the mobile bundle.
9. Export and deletion can be initiated and their status/retention behavior is documented.
10. Core journeys work with screen reader, enlarged text, reduced motion, and tested iOS/Android layouts.
11. Pricing/entitlements are disclosed before checkout and billing passes current store policy review before release.
12. Privacy notice, terms, support process, incident runbook, and Ghana applicability review are complete before public launch.
